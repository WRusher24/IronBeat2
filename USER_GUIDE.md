## 1. Create your account

1. Open IronBeat and tap **Create an account**.
2. Enter a name, email and password (8+ characters).
3. Tap **Create my vault**.

That's it — no confirmation email, no approval queue. You are inside your vault immediately.

Everything you do afterwards lives in your account only: folders, saved tracks, search chips and play history. Another IronBeat user cannot see any of it, and there is no shared or public space.

**Forgot your password?** There is no reset flow yet — accounts are self-contained by design. Keep the password somewhere safe, or delete the account from **Account → Delete account** and start fresh.

## 2. Add IronBeat to your iPhone home screen

The web version works, but the installed version is the point: fullscreen, no address bar, lock-screen controls.

1. Open IronBeat in **Safari**.
   > Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot install home-screen web apps. Safari only.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) in the toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Name it **IronBeat** and tap **Add**.
5. Launch it from the home screen.

You will notice:

- No browser chrome — the app fills the screen.
- The cream status bar blends into the interface.
- Lock-screen and AirPods controls work.
- Your session stays signed in for 30 days.

**On Android (Chrome):** menu → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. The same standalone behaviour applies.

**Updating:** the app refreshes itself when online. If a new version seems missing, close it fully from the app switcher and reopen.

## 3. Search instantly

The search bar sits at the top of the home screen — thumb height, no scrolling required.

1. Tap the bar and type. Two characters is enough to start.
2. Results appear in about a second, pulled from Audius and the Internet Archive at the same time.
3. Tap any result.

**What happens when you tap:** the track starts immediately **and the rest of the result list becomes your queue**. When the track ends, the next one plays on its own. You never have to touch the phone between sets.

Under the result count you can see which catalogues answered — for example `Audius · Archive.org`. If one is missing, the other still works.

**Every result is a complete track.** IronBeat only uses sources that hand over the whole song, so nothing stops at thirty seconds and nothing cuts out when you lock the phone.

**Useful queries**

| Goal | Try |
| --- | --- |
| High-energy | `brazilian phonk`, `drift phonk`, `hard techno` |
| Heavy lifting | `hard rock workout`, `metal lifting`, `industrial` |
| Focus | `nasheed`, `cinematic focus`, `oud instrumental` |
| Cardio | `drum and bass 174`, `deep house cardio` |
| Wind-down | `lofi`, `ambient piano`, `stretching` |

Press **Enter** (or tap **Play all**) to start the entire result list from the top.

## 4. Recent chips — one-tap pump

Below the search bar is a horizontal row of your past **successful** searches.

Tap a chip and IronBeat re-runs that search and starts playing the first result. No typing, no picking — one tap, music.

- Chips only appear for searches that actually returned audio, so they always work.
- The most recent sits first; the row holds your last eight.
- Chips are private to your account.
- To clear them, swipe the row to the end — clearing is available from the account screen in the current build, or simply let them rotate out.

## 6. Smart Shuffle

The dark card at the top of the home screen builds a one-off mix from **your** behaviour:

- the genres you search for most
- the artists you actually play

Tap **Start the mix**. You get roughly forty tracks, shuffled, with the signals it used shown as chips underneath. Every shuffle is different.

A brand-new account has no history yet, so the first mix uses starter genres. Search and play a few things — the mix sharpens quickly.

## 7. Build a training folder

Folders are how you keep a session's soundtrack together.

**Create one**

1. Tap **Folders** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap **＋** (top right) or the dashed **New folder** card.
3. Name it after the session, not the song — "Push day — heavy", "Leg day", "Meet prep".
4. Optional: add a note and pick an accent colour.
5. Tap **Create folder**.

**Add tracks**

1. Search for something.
2. Tap **＋** on the result row.
3. Pick a folder — or tap **New training folder** to create one on the spot.
4. The track is saved with its artwork, artist and stream URL.

**Manage**

Open a folder to:

- **Play all** — queue the whole folder from the top
- **Shuffle** — same tracks, random order
- **⋯ on any row** — move up, move down, or remove
- **⚙ top right** — rename, re-note, recolour, or delete the folder

Folders are ordered by most recently updated, so whatever you touched last is first.

## 8. Saving tracks

Tap the **♡** on any row. The track goes to **Saved** in the bottom bar — one tap away next session, no searching again.

Tap **♡** again to remove it. Saving is instant and works offline for anything already in your vault.

## 9. The player

**Mini player.** Once something is playing, a bar sits above the bottom navigation on every screen: artwork, title, play/pause, skip, and a thin progress line. It follows you from Home to Search to Folders without interrupting playback.

**Full player.** Tap the artwork or title in the mini player.

- Drag the scrubber to seek
- **⤨** shuffle · **⟲** repeat (off → all → one)
- **Up next** shows the queue; tap any row to jump to it
- **↗** opens the original source (Audius or the Internet Archive)

**The queue survives.** Close the app, lock the phone, come back an hour later — the last track, its position and the queue are restored. It comes back paused, so nothing blasts in the locker room.

## 10. Lock screen & AirPods controls

IronBeat declares itself as a media player to iOS, so the audio keeps running when you lock the phone, switch apps, or pocket the device.

- **Lock screen** — artwork, title, artist, scrubber, play/pause, skip.
- **AirPods** — double-tap plays/pauses; double-tap-and-hold skips (per your iOS settings).
- **Control Centre** — the same controls appear in the Now Playing widget.
- **CarPlay / Bluetooth** — standard transport commands are forwarded to the app.

Under the hood this uses three web standards together: the **Audio Session API** (`navigator.audioSession.type = "playback"`, which tells iOS this is long-form media rather than a sound effect), the **Media Session API** (metadata and transport handlers), and a DOM-attached `<audio>` element with `playsinline` so the stream isn't suspended.

No setup needed. If the controls don't appear, make sure the app was installed from Safari and that a track is actually playing.

## 11. What happens offline

Streaming needs a connection — tracks are not downloaded to the device.

When you lose signal:

- Playback stops at the current buffer.
- Opening the app shows a friendly **"Signal dropped"** screen instead of a browser error.
- Your folders, saved tracks and queue are intact and reappear the moment you reconnect.

---

## 12. Your privacy

- Your vault is yours alone. Every folder, track, chip and play is tied to your account and filtered by it on every request.
- Sessions last 30 days, are stored in an `httpOnly` cookie, and end immediately when you sign out.
- **Account → Delete account** erases the account and everything attached to it, permanently. It asks you to type `DELETE` first.
- IronBeat has no analytics, no trackers and no advertising.

## 13. Tips from the floor

**Set up before you start.** Queue your folder, then put the phone face down. The mini player and lock screen are all you need.

**Use chips for warm-ups.** The same three searches every session become three taps instead of three typed queries.

**Let a mood carry the session.** Tap a card at the start and don't touch it again — the queue is long enough for a full workout.

**Build one folder per session type**, not per genre. "Push day" is easier to find mid-set than "phonk 2024".

**Lock the phone and keep lifting.** Every track plays in the background — the app tells iOS it is a media player, so the screen going dark doesn't stop the music.

**Re-run Smart Shuffle when a session feels flat.** It re-reads your history each time, so it drifts as your taste does.
